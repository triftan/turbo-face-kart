On a phone: drag anywhere on the left half to steer. Your kart speeds up by itself. Hold DRIFT through a turn and let go for a turbo, tap ITEM to fire, hold BRAKE to slow down or reverse, tap SWAP to switch items, and tap II to pause.

Hold accelerate during the "1" of the countdown for a rocket start. Yellow arrow pads give a free boost. Gamepads work too (A gas, B brake, RB drift, LB or X item).
