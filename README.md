# Turbo Face Kart

A 3D kart racer in one HTML file. Upload a photo and it becomes your driver's face. No build step, no libraries, and nothing to download beyond the file. The graphics, sound, music and tracks are all generated in code.

## Play

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. It needs WebGL2, which every current browser has.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrows or WASD | Drive |
| Shift or Space | Hop and drift. Let go for a turbo |
| E or Enter | Use item |
| Q | Swap items |
| Esc or P | Pause |
| M | Mute |
| F | Show frame rate |

Gamepads work too.

## What's in it

- 4 worlds, from easy to hard: Sunny Meadow, Dune Canyon, Frost Peak, Volcano Rush
- 4 karts with different stats, and 8 paint colours
- A starting weapon and boost you choose, plus random items from `?` boxes
- Finish in the top 3 to win
- Best times saved per world in your browser (local storage)
